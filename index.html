Our Story

Welcome to Elegant Lodge, Galle

Located within 2.7 km of Bonavista Beach and 1.4 km of Galle International Cricket Stadium, Elegant Lodge provides rooms with air conditioning and a private bathroom in the beautiful coastal city of Galle.

Featuring a relaxing terrace, our property is set a short distance from the Dutch Church Galle, Galle Fort and the Galle Fort National Museum. We are 2.3 km from Galle Lighthouse, and within less than 1 km of the city centre.

Guest rooms are equipped with a kettle and a dining table, and guests can enjoy a delicious Full English/Irish or Asian breakfast to start the day.
